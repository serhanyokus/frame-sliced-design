# Block: screen moment

An app screen appears over the footage as it is mentioned.

## Behavior
1. The phone card enters small. Choose its position based on the speaker's gesture (the corner they point to); if there is no gesture, use the left side because platform controls occupy the right rail.
2. If the screen needs to be read, bring it to the center and enlarge it (about 1.4 scale) after roughly 0.5 seconds. Do not zoom into the phone screen; enlarge the phone itself.
3. Move captions below the phone.
4. Hide the card when the sentence ends.

## Rules
- Use real product screens only. Capture a missing screen from the app; do not present a redrawn screen as a real one.
- Personal details on screen should match the spoken line (for example, a name).
- Covering the face for too long slows the pace; aim for no more than about 6 seconds at a time.

## Techniques used

Motion (enlargement), sound design (click), and captions (repositioning).
