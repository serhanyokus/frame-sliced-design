# Safe zones

Approximate values for a 1080×1920 frame. Keep important text and screens outside these areas.

| Area | Why |
|---|---|
| Right rail (x > ~930) | TikTok/Reels like, comment, and share controls |
| Bottom ~15% (y > ~1600) | Description, username, and audio information |
| Top ~8% (y < ~150) | “Following / For You” tabs and live indicator |

Default caption position: centered around y ≈ 1360. When a large phone screen appears, captions move below it without entering the bottom unsafe area.
