# Decision priority

Adapted from Walter Murch's “Rule of Six” for social video. When two decisions conflict, the higher item on this list wins.

1. **Message:** Is the idea clear?
2. **Emotion and engagement:** Does the viewer feel something and stay engaged?
3. **Rhythm:** Does the flow hold attention? Does any part drag?
4. **Readability:** Can it be read on a phone, with sound off, under the platform interface?
5. **Brand:** Are color, voice, and series identity consistent?
6. **Decoration:** Effects, transitions, and decorative motion

Example: if an app screen looks beautiful but cannot be read on a phone, enlarge the screen (4) and sacrifice decoration (6).
