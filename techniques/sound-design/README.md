# Technique: sound design

## Voice
- Apply light noise reduction (high-pass around 70 Hz and gentle FFT denoising), soft compression, and a target of −16 LUFS.
- Extend cuts until the sound has decayed; do not clip word endings.

## Music
- Use original or licensed music. Do not use unlicensed tracks.
- Target about −36 LUFS under speech and −28 LUFS in gaps (automatic ducking).
- Leave about 0.3 seconds of music silence before an important sentence.

## Effects

| Visible action | Sound |
|---|---|
| Tap | Click |
| Card or screen appears | Light click |
| Large emphasis or label | Pop |
| Calendar or paper movement | Paper sound |
| Transition | Swoosh |
| Series stamp | Sparkling bell arpeggio |
