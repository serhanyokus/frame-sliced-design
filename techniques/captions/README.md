# Technique: captions

- Break captions into 2–3 word groups, at punctuation or around 16 characters.
- Highlight the spoken word in white inside a brand-color box.
- Entrance: slide gently up from below in about 0.14 seconds, without bouncing.
- Style: bold type (Inter ExtraBold at about 66 px) with a dark outline.
- Hide captions during large emphasis; reposition them when a phone screen appears.
- Time captions at word level using a transcript with word timestamps.
