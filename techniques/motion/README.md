# Technique: motion

- **Slow push:** slowly zoom from 1.0 to about 1.07 during an important sentence.
- **Punch-in:** a quick, small zoom (about 1.10–1.12) at an emphasis moment.
- **Hide a cut:** alternate between normal and slightly closer framing across adjacent pieces so a jump cut feels like a camera change.
- **Opening:** settle from about 1.15 to the normal framing over 0.5 seconds.
- **Enlarge a screen:** see [screen moment](../../blocks/screen-moment/README.md).
- Center zooms around face level (about 34% of frame height).
