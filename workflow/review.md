# Review

- **Watch on a phone with sound:** check the rhythm and music level; do effects overpower the voice?
- **Watch on a phone without sound:** can the story be understood from text alone?
- **Readability:** does every line and screen stay on long enough to be read?
- **Safe zones:** is anything hidden by platform controls?
- **Director's eye:** does the hook land in the first second? Is the face covered for too long? Does the pace sag?
- **Delivery:** 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264; keep the shareable copy under 30 MB.

✅ After final approval: save the video and update `log.md` and `learning/`.
